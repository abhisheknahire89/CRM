# Decisions log

Every place where the deck (`input/Evidence-native CRM - Abhishek Nahire.pptx`) or the brief left something open, and what was chosen. Rule used throughout: **the reading most consistent with the deck wins.** Nothing below changes the product strategy.

| ID | Ambiguity | Decision | Why / deck reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D-01** | The PPT was not at `/input` | Copied from `~/Downloads/Evidence-native CRM - Abhishek Nahire.pptx` into `/input`. All 16 slides read first (core 1–8, backup B1–B8). | Brief: "READ IT FIRST" |
| **D-02** | An earlier concept image (`Why an Evidence-Native CRM_.png`) exists in Downloads and shows Castellan facts that differ (a *supported* "customer deadline Nov 20"; security "contradicted"). | **Ignored.** The deck and the brief are authoritative. Two dates were reused only because they do not conflict: rep note **24 Sep**, procurement email **2 Oct**. | Slide 3 and the brief fix the four claims |
| **D-03** | Is a champion's statement "evidence" for procurement? | **Context, not support.** Admissible = customer-side source **and** speaker in the rule's accepted authorities. The champion's "two weeks" is kept in history, labelled *Superseded*, and cannot support the claim alone. | Slide 12: "Procurement outranks the champion on this claim"; slide 3: "Champion said so; a later procurement email disagrees" |
| **D-04** | "5–8 predefined claims" vs the four Castellan claims | The RevOps starter set has **6** definitions (the four, plus *Economic buyer engaged* and *Legal review started*). Demo deals carry the four. The two extra appear only on the RevOps screen, labelled "not used on the demo deals". | Slide 7 ("5–8 predefined claims"); slide 3 ("four claims RevOps chose to inspect") |
| **D-05** | Does STALE queue a deal? | **No.** Queue = Commit or Best Case with ≥ 1 UNSUPPORTED or CONTRADICTED claim, literally. STALE is displayed on the deal and in the summary line. | Slide 5 (manager sees "unsupported or contradicted") |
| **D-06** | How is "Evidence review priority" computed? (Renamed from "Review priority" in the final refinement, D-25.) Slide 4 lists it as an output without a formula. | Deterministic: **HIGH** = Commit and (≥ 1 contradicted or ≥ 2 unsupported); **MEDIUM** = (Commit and 1 unsupported) or (Best Case and (≥ 1 contradicted or ≥ 2 unsupported)); else **LOW**. Sort: priority → flagged claims → contradicted → deal value. Labelled illustrative. | Slide 4 ("Review priority" is a policy output). Not a prediction; no outcome data used |
| **D-07** | What does "supports" mean for a duration or a date? | `EQUALS` for booleans. `AT_MOST` for procurement weeks and close date: customer evidence **no worse than** the CRM supports it; worse contradicts. Close date reads as "close **by** 15 Oct". | Slide 3 wording "Close by 15 Oct" |
| **D-08** | Precedence between two admissible items | Deck says "later **or** more authoritative". Implemented literally: a disagreeing item overrides a supporting one if it is later **or** higher authority. Older, lower-authority disagreement is shown in history but does not override. A contradiction is **not** softened by age. | Slide 12; precision-before-recall |
| **D-09** | Where does extraction confidence go? | Shown on every AI-extracted passage and **never** an input to `computeClaimStatus` (tested). In production, a tuned threshold may stop a low-confidence *candidate* from being stored as evidence at ingestion (a precision control in the AI layer). Policy never reads the score. The prototype applies no gating. | Slide 4: "Extraction confidence ≠ business status" |
| **D-10** | Can a rep's seller-supplied context (formerly "attached evidence") move a status? | **No.** Seller-supplied context is stored, shown, and labelled *seller-supplied*; it is never admissible (nor is the rep's CRM note). If the customer then confirms in a connected source, the next sync recomputes. | Slide 13 / B5 ("marked as seller-supplied"); slide 3 (rep note is not customer evidence) |
| **D-11** | What does a rep "dispute" do? | Logged as a rep response, shown to the manager beside the claim, and (in production) added to the evaluation set. It does **not** rewrite the status. | Slide 12: "People's decisions are logged beside the status and never rewrite it" |
| **D-12** | What can the manager decide? | Three logged decisions: *Keep Forecast = {current}*, *Plan to move Forecast to {next lower}*, *Ask the rep to verify*. All are **records**. The ledger never changes the CRM forecast (read-only); the manager changes it in the CRM. | Slide 7 (read-only); slide 3 ("the forecast remains the manager's call") |
| **D-13** | Dates | Demo clock fixed at **8 Oct 2026, 09:00 UTC** (the date of the deck's sources); flags surfaced to the rep **7 Oct 16:00**. CFO email **17 Sep** is from the brief; every other date is invented. | Brief §Castellan; slide 5 ("the day before the review") |
| **D-14** | Rep-first needs to be *visible* | One rep response is seeded on Castellan Security ("will verify with customer", 7 Oct 17:20) so the manager view shows the rep acted first. Procurement is left open so the live demo can show the rep actions. | Slide 5, B7 |
| **D-15** | Other deals | Six more synthetic deals; five are queued, **one (Vantor Cargo) has all four claims supported and is deliberately absent** from the queue, which demonstrates the filter. All five statuses appear somewhere in the data. | Brief: "approximately 5–6 synthetic deals" |
| **D-16** | Roles in the prototype | Manager (`/`), Rep (`/rep`), RevOps (`/revops`) via a role toggle. The CRO / VP Sales is the buyer, not a prototype role. | Slide 2 |
| **D-17** | Demo reason text | The decision reason is prefilled with the brief's example and marked "Prefilled for the demo. Edit freely." Reason is required (≥ 10 chars). | Brief §Screen 4; slide 4 ("decision + reason") |
| **D-18** | No backend | Static Next.js export. `LocalEvidenceRepository` implements the `EvidenceRepository` interface used by the Engineering Approach; decisions persist in `localStorage`; **Reset demo** clears them. | Brief: "no backend required … shortcuts must be replaceable" |
| **D-19** | RevOps screen: editable or not? | Only **freshness windows** are editable (so the deterministic effect is demonstrable: Budget → 10 days turns Castellan Budget STALE). Authorities are shown, ordered, read-only. No rule builder. | Slide 7 ("Custom rules engine" is *not yet*) |
| **D-20** | Vendor names | Generic labels ("CRM", "Email", "Call recorder"). The deck says "Salesforce **or** HubSpot, one call recorder" without choosing. | Slide 7 |
| **D-21** | Architecture weight | Modular monolith + one Postgres + object store + a job queue. No vector DB, graph DB, Kafka, microservices or agent framework, each with an explicit trigger for when it would be added. | Brief §Section 2 |
| **D-22** | Evaluation design | 50 closed deals × ~8 claims ≈ 400 claim evaluations, judged **point-in-time** (as at that week's forecast review, not at close), by two labellers with adjudication. Thresholds are **proposed**, not benchmarks. | Slide 11 / B3: "Judge each claim as it stood at that week's forecast review, not at close" |
| **D-23** | "Not yet" items on screen | Appear only in the RevOps screen's "Not in V1 · future possibilities" card and in docs, labelled as future. Nothing in the UI implies them. | Slide 7 |
| **D-24** | Evidence age wording | "Fresh for N days" is inclusive: evidence exactly N days old is fresh; N+1 is stale. | Slide 12 ("older than the window") |
| **D-25** | Final refinement: naming of the queue's priority | Renamed **"Review priority" → "Evidence review priority"** everywhere in the prototype, with the note "Based on evidence status only — not a win probability."
